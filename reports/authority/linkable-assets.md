## Publication gate

Before an asset is published, record its owner, source files, test date, product version, intended query or audience, canonical URL, attribution plan, and rollback or correction path. Do not create a new indexable URL unless the search-intent, unique value, product connection, attribution plan, and measurement owner are documented.

## Measurement

Measure referring domains, linked pages, branded-search change, referral visits, tool completions, and App Store outbound clicks. Do not treat impressions, page count, or a link alone as product acquisition.
