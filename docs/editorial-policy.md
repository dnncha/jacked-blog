# Editorial policy

Jacked training and product guidance should be useful, technically clear, and honest about what has been tested.

## Authorship and review

Every factual article must identify a real author. Add a reviewer only when a real person with relevant experience has reviewed the article. Do not invent names, credentials, affiliations, testing records, or first-hand experience. Until those fields are supplied truthfully, the article is not presented as expert-reviewed.

## Sources and method

Articles that make scientific, health, product-comparison, or import-compatibility claims must list the sources used, explain the method where a calculation or evaluation is involved, and state material limitations. Sources must support the visible claim; a citation list is not a substitute for checking the claim.

## Dates and corrections

`publishedAt` records the first publication date. `updatedAt` changes only after a substantive review or correction. A deployment alone is not an update. Material corrections should be recorded with the corrected claim, source, and date.

## Product and comparison claims

Describe only features present in the current shipping app or clearly label planned work. When Jacked is compared with another product, disclose that Jacked is the developer, date the test, record the version and device, distinguish observation from opinion, and avoid claims that cannot be reproduced.

## Health and medical boundaries

Training information is not medical advice. Health-sensitive topics require careful sourcing, restrained language, and review by a genuinely qualified person when the article presents clinical or safety guidance. Do not turn a general article into a diagnosis or treatment recommendation.

## Search and URL policy

Do not create indexable pages solely by substituting an exercise, location, competitor, or keyword into a shared template. A new URL needs a documented intent, distinct value, product connection, attribution plan, measurement owner, and rollback path. Preserve existing URLs until search, link, canonical, and user-value evidence has been checked.
