# Press and review methodology

This document defines how Jacked describes the product and evaluates third-party coverage.

## Product facts

Use the currently shipping iPhone app and the public App Store listing as the source of truth. Before publishing a fact, record the app version, device or simulator used, operating-system version, date tested, and the relevant workflow. Do not describe an unshipped feature as available.

## Independent evaluation

An evaluation should state:

- which version and date were tested;
- the device, operating system, and test data used;
- the workflows completed and any limitations;
- which statements are direct observations and which are reviewer opinion;
- that Jacked is the product developer when the developer is comparing its own product.

Screenshots and recordings must show real product behaviour, use anonymised test data, and respect the rights and terms of any third-party product shown. Do not alter a result to make the product appear stronger.

## Editorial and outreach rules

Coverage must be earned through a useful asset or a relevant product evaluation. Do not purchase links, automate community posting, send mass unsolicited messages, fabricate reviews or testimonials, hide incentives, or continue contacting someone who declines. Any incentive must be disclosed by the recipient and must not depend on a positive opinion.

## Corrections

If a published fact becomes inaccurate, update the page with the substantive update date and explain the correction where it changes the reader's understanding. Keep a short record of the old claim, corrected claim, source, and date.

## Scope

This is a methodology, not evidence that a review, link, creator relationship, or product test exists. Those records must be added from source-backed work.
