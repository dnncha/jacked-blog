What actually makes shoulders look wider.

Width comes mostly from the side delts. Pressing trains the front of the shoulder; the side and rear need their own work.

1

Side delts carry the width

Lateral raises train the part of the shoulder that sticks out past your arms from the front. They lead the shoulder work in a Wider shoulders block.

2

Rear delts finish the look

Rear delts shape the shoulder from the side and back. A rear-delt fly or face pull keeps the whole cap growing, not just the front.

3

Proof from three angles

Shoulders change from every side. Frame Check takes matched front, side and back photos each time, so the comparison is fair.

Questions about building wider shoulders.

How many sets a week does it take to build wider shoulders?

Research on weekly volume favours roughly 10 or more hard sets per muscle a week for growth (Schoenfeld, Ogborn and Krieger, 2017). Surpass starter plans give each priority muscle a second exercise on the days that train it, aiming for about 10 to 20 hard sets a week, and Today shows how many are left.

Which exercises should I use?

Lateral raises (dumbbell, cable or machine) for the side delts, a rear-delt fly or face pull for the rear delts, and one overhead press. High-rep raises taken close to failure work well here.

Will overhead pressing alone make my shoulders wider?

Pressing mostly trains the front delts. Side delts get some work from it, but direct lateral raises are the most reliable way to add width.

Does Surpass rate my physique from a photo?

No. Frame Check keeps matched photos on your phone and asks you to mark what you see. There is no score and no comparison with anyone else, only with your own past check-ins.
