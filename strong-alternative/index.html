Questions about moving from Strong.

How do I export workouts from Strong on iPhone?

Open Strong, go to Settings, and select Export Strong Data. Save the spreadsheet-friendly CSV to Files, then choose Strong on the Surpass import screen.

Does Surpass support every Strong export language?

Surpass currently supports English Strong exports. Keep the original export unchanged so its headers and dates can be checked correctly.

What Strong data can Surpass read?

Supported fields include workout dates and names, exercise names, set order, weight and units, reps, RPE, distance, duration, and notes when those fields are present.

Does Surpass need my Strong login?

No. Surpass imports the CSV file you select on your iPhone and does not request access to your Strong account.

Can I undo a Strong import?

Yes. Imported sessions and their derived history can be removed separately without deleting workouts you logged manually in Surpass.
