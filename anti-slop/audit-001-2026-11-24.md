# antislop audit 001: jacked.coach (jacked-blog), 24 November 2026

Mode: AFTER, with HIGH and MEDIUM findings approved for fixing before the audit started.
Scope: the Next.js site in `app/`, `public/home.css` and `public/manifest.json`. The 262 long-form
articles in `content/blog/` were scanned for copy rules only.

Direction: there is no `DESIGN.md` in this repo. The site follows the Surpass app's own identity
(near-black ground, one gold accent, small uppercase tracked labels, the same screenshots as the App
Store listing). No redesign was attempted. Read as: product site and training library for iPhone
lifters, dark editorial style, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.
